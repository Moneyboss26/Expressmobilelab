Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What identification do I need for my appointment?

All patients must present a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (such as a driver's license, state ID, or passport) at the time of the visit. All information on your ID—specifically your full legal name and date of birth—must match your doctor's lab order before blood can be drawn.

How do I get my doctor's order to you?

Have your doctor's office email your signed lab order directly to expressmobilelab@mail.com. Receiving the order straight from your provider ensures everything is accurate and verified before we schedule your visit.

Can I schedule an appointment before my doctor sends the order?

No. A valid physician's lab order must be received directly from your provider prior to scheduling your appointment.

How do I pay for my mobile visit?

You can pay with cash at the time of your appointment or by credit card via invoice. Your health insurance card is still provided to the testing laboratory (such as LabCorp or Quest) to cover the actual lab analysis.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

You may cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 4 hours before your scheduled arrival window. Cancellations made with less than 4 hours' notice, missed appointments, or inability to complete a draw due to a missing order or invalid/mismatched ID will forfeit the travel fee. Privacy Policy & HIPAA Confidentiality Notice

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request a mobile blood draw or your physician submits a lab order, we collect only the information necessary to perform your service: your name, date of birth, government ID verification, contact details, service address, ordering physician's information, and the laboratory requisition.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Information

We strictly safeguard your personal and health information in accordance with HIPAA privacy standards. Your information is used solely to verify your identity and physician's order, schedule your appointment, process invoicing, and deliver your labeled specimen to your designated testing laboratory (such as Quest Diagnostics or LabCorp).

3. Information Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or medical information. Data is shared only with your ordering healthcare provider and the designated testing laboratory performing your analysis.

4. Contact Us

For privacy questions or records requests, contact us at expressmobilelab@mail.com or call 1-800-282-9033.

Terms & Conditions

1. Scope of Service

Express Mobile Lab operates solely as an NPI-registered mobile specimen collection service performed by an NHA-Certified Medical Assistant. We do not practice medicine, offer medical consultations, or interpret test results.

2. Mandatory Physician Order

A valid, signed laboratory order must be sent directly from your licensed healthcare provider to expressmobilelab@mail.com before any appointment will be confirmed or performed.

3. Payment Terms

Mobile collection and travel fees are separate from laboratory testing charges. Payment is due via cash at the time of service or credit card via invoice prior to/at the time of service. We do not bill health insurance for mobile fees.

4. Patient Identification & Safe Environment

Patients must present a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID upon arrival, and all patient information (including full legal name and date of birth) must match the physician's lab order exactly before specimens can be collected. Patients should also have their health insurance card available for the testing lab's billing records and provide a clean, well-lit, smoke-free, and safe environment (with pets secured) during the visit.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Cancellations & Rescheduling (4+ Hours Notice)

Appointments canceled or rescheduled at least 4 hours prior to the scheduled arrival window will receive a 100% refund of any prepaid invoice fees at no penalty.

2. Late Cancellations & No-Shows

If an appointment is canceled with less than 4 hours' notice, or if our specialist arrives at the location and cannot complete the draw due to patient absence, missing valid government ID, mismatched order information, unsafe conditions, or patient refusal, the travel fee is non-refundable (or will be billed via invoice).

3. Unsuccessful Venipuncture

In the rare event a blood draw cannot be completed after two standard venipuncture attempts due to vein accessibility, the mobile draw fee will be refunded or credited toward a future attempt, while the travel fee remains non-refundable to cover mileage and transit time.

4. Completed Services

Once blood collection has been completed and delivered to the designated laboratory or courier, all mobile draw and travel fees are final and non-refundable.
